Our Journey Since 1995

PureHeal Hospital began with a dream in 1995 — to make world-class healthcare reachable for every Indian. What started as a 50-bed clinic in a small town has grown into an 800-bed super-specialty hospital chain across 5 states.

Founded by Dr. Aryan Sharma (Cardiac Surgeon) and Dr. Priya Mehta (Neurologist), we believed that healing is 50% medicine and 50% love. Today, our 2500+ doctors and nurses live this belief every day.

From performing the country's first robotic heart surgery in 2010 to running 500+ free rural health camps, we've stayed true to our roots while embracing AI diagnostics, telemedicine, and 24/7 emergency care.
